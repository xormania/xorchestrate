## Findings ordered by decision impact

### M01 — The strong comparator is appropriate, but perfect performance is conditional on supplied structure

The model-visible packet includes the invariant name, left operand, operator, right operand, explicit observation flag and expected/observed subject binding. The key computes truth using these operands, and P0 applies essentially the same relational contract (`probes.py:51-65`, `corpus.py:52-77`, `policies.py:47-140`). P0's probe selector also has explicit family-specific dispatch, including operand-type distinctions for context versus replay and attempted-delivery versus effect recovery (`policies.py:99-115`). Runtime P0 does not read hidden labels/case IDs/splits. It is nevertheless authored with the known family vocabulary, not a learned extractor facing new incident semantics.

This does not make P0 an unfair comparator. The assignment explicitly says numerical comparisons, identifiers, ordering and known policy checks should remain in code (`spec/01-EXECUTION-PROMPT.md`, Implement one experiment); a weak lexical comparator would manufacture value. It means the principal result is about assessment over already extracted operands. The extraction and normalization work that produces that representation, and its error rate/cost on operational incidents, are absent. The key and comparator's shared contract construction should be disclosed when interpreting 24/24 as evidence strength: the raw controlled executions support the operands, but this is not an independent incident-discovery benchmark.

Minimal post-hoc reproduction with controls: preserve the complete raw-observation section in the healthy and fault authority packets, but remove only `facts.checks`. P0 changes from accept/reject respectively to investigate/investigate. Reversing check order leaves accept/reject unchanged. No Jev call was made on these ablations; they show P0's structural dependency, not Jev superiority. Original historical outcomes remain untouched. The original authority fault includes raw `executed_context.actor=final-actor` versus `observed_original_actor=old-actor`, so the original raw evidence remains readable to a semantic evaluator even though P0 intentionally cannot reconstruct arbitrary checks.

Minimum additional evidence: independently sampled, redacted operational packets with the intended real evidence contract, predeclared extraction methods for every policy, extraction errors and labor/token/runtime costs, and a held-out evaluation of the whole pipeline. Do not replace structured controls with a weak baseline merely to create a model win.

### M02 — Investigation gains cannot be assigned to new observations in P4; the available packet already solves the task

The main report correctly discloses that the frozen structured baseline over already available P3 packets yields 24/24 final actions without new calls (`REPORT.md:65`). The implementation marks every applicable executed probe `distinguishing=True` (`probes.py:375-376`), and `evaluation.py:199-225` turns that into `information_gain_observed` without comparing the prior evidence. This flag measures an executed discriminating boundary in isolation, not incremental information to this decision. It is a measurement-label overclaim in machine-readable summaries, even though the prose now qualifies it.

P4 switches from Jev interpretation to `baseline(updated)` after a further probe (`runner.py:228-235`). Its action improvements therefore combine re-execution and an interpreter change. The post-hoc supplement also uses a structured interpreter and a different candidate selector. Additional observations and execution costs are real; their causal contribution beyond interpreting facts already present is not established. In contrast, P0_probe's eight initially missing cases do acquire previously withheld decisive operands; that is genuine evidence acquisition under the stated contract, though all eight happen to end adversely.

Minimum additional evidence: an explicit factorial or paired ablation at the same frozen pre-investigation state: old interpreter/no new observation; old interpreter/new observation; structured interpreter/no new observation; structured interpreter/new observation. Match candidate availability and account for failures. No new paid calls were authorized or made in this review.

### M03 — The zero-call random arm is valid as a degenerate reasoning control, insufficient as total investigation-allocation evidence

Original P4 selected zero reasoning calls. `runner.py:309-316` samples exactly that reasoning count for the random policy; random equals never-reason. This is neither a fabricated arm nor a randomizer failure. It simply does not answer whether Jev allocated its eleven extra probes better than a non-Jev route at matched total investigation cost.

The supplemental five-probe comparison is useful exploratory evidence and honestly retains the original arm. Its post-hoc plan freezes the next actions after the main outputs are known; it is not a prespecified holdout rescue. Candidate selection, interpreter, time, prefix costs, and possibly compute price differ. One seed and clustered cases cannot establish that random routing generally wins. The matched-call supplement can demonstrate those particular composite policy outcomes; it cannot isolate the causal contribution of Jev's route assessment. E03 explicitly permits a clear call-count proxy with disclosed cost mismatch, and the supplement supplies that proxy. The comparison inventory can therefore be demonstrated within its bounded scope. The important unverified inference is isolated routing advantage, not the existence or validity of these measured arms.

The current corpus also lacks a demonstrated hard-inference stratum where the best next observation is unavailable and a reasoning evaluator is needed. Once P3 observations are merged, structured checks suffice everywhere. A zero-reasoning outcome on this set is therefore not by itself a failure of the implementation or proof that reasoning is worthless. The authority cases do establish a missed correction opportunity conditional on the flawed P3 stack, but an even simpler interpreter also fixes them.

Minimum additional evidence: prospective common candidates and evidence; randomized routing with matched expected money or clearly declared call/time budgets and mismatch; clean isolation of route versus candidate versus interpretation; several seeds/repeated runs treated as repeated measurements; and separately designed hard-inference, missing-observation, ambiguous-policy and unsupported-candidate cases.

### M04 — Both confident false clears are supported errors, but belong to one dependency group

The two P4 false clears are `cef7128badec1` and `c51eabfc2dae5`, both authority group `gbc4c9743d8e9`. The former starts with decisive `final-actor != old-actor` and `final-work != old-work` and is falsely accepted from P1 through P4. The latter begins correctly unresolved at P1, receives exactly those decisive contradictions through its executed P2 probe, then becomes a false clear. P3 judgment confidences 0.95 and 0.85 survive into P4; both have `route=none`. These are two related case outcomes, not two independent operational incidents.

Both lie in audit stratum `accept:none:high`, whose population is four and sample is two. Neither was selected. Under the retained stratified simple-random-sampling design, the probability of missing both known errors in that stratum is `C(2,2)/C(4,2)=1/6`. This is an exact conditional calculation for this frozen controlled population, not an estimated production rate. The zero Horvitz–Thompson point estimate is compatible with the observed two population errors and does not show sampler malfunction. Nonzero inclusion probability is demonstrated; sufficient rare-error protection is not.

Minimum additional evidence: operationally representative, grouped uncertainty analysis and adequate sample sizes; consequence-sensitive audit design tested on confidently accepted failures; preserve random routine-estimation versus targeted fault-search streams. No confidence threshold alone can be inferred safe from this pilot.

### M05 — Stage-specific keys are supported; the presentation retains stale missing declarations and needs a representation control

The offline check matched all 24 initial and all 24 outcome local judgments by direct independent operator comparisons. Initial cases are 8 supports, 8 contradicts, 8 unresolved. All eight unresolved cases later contradict. All 72 main case requests contain the exact bounded initial packet, with no forbidden label/split/group/fault selector fields in payloads. For the 42 post-probe/adversarial requests with an executed observation, independently interpreting the supplied new observation matches the outcome key. This supports the actual stage transition; it is not a proof against every possible semantic leakage path.

Models receive the initial packet, including its initial `declared_missing` text, plus a separately named `new_probe_observation` (`runner.py:212-217`, `contracts.py:116-132`). The merged P3 available packet also retains `facts.declared_missing` after checks become observed because `merge_observation` updates rather than replaces that dictionary (`policies.py:143-155`). This is a representation inconsistency worth controlling, especially for model-versus-code comparisons: P0 ignores that prose and trusts the explicit latest comparison fields.

It does not justify changing the outcome key or recoding a false accept as correct. In authority request `ae6146ca349d25e57b29812537763f53cbdcb6205960dc12aeb728bc0ed0f578`, `new_probe_observation.status=executed` includes explicit bound actor/work contradictions; the task asks to reassess supplied observations. Moreover, the initially decisive authority fault has no missing declaration and is also falsely cleared. Stale missing text therefore cannot explain both errors, and this offline review cannot causally attribute either error to it. Six of the eight initially missing cases are correctly rejected by P3 despite the retained text, one remains unresolved, and one false-clears.

Minimum additional evidence: paired initial-plus-delta versus clean current-view representations with identical substantive observations, clear temporal/precedence rules, no hidden truth, and exact model configuration. This could narrow the negative interpretation of Jev without rewriting the measured run.

### M06 — Correlation breadth, robustness breadth and case independence remain limited

No dependency group crosses development/calibration/holdout in the frozen corpus. There are 2/1/4 groups respectively (6/6/12 case rows). Arguments and attempts share one group. Holdout trajectories change known schedules, not unknown mechanisms (`corpus.py:165-176`, `CORPUS.md:17-30`). This supports a limited known-family transfer check, not discovery or operational prevalence.

Relationship testing is three related views of replay, with explicit revision operands. No positive common-implementation-cause control is present. Broad failure correlation is unverified even if every local relationship key is correct. Claim/violation semantics rightly distinguish an unsatisfied supplied claim from a product defect: receipt controls reject forged completion claims, so an inferred obligation violation does not prove live misdelivery.

Robustness generation takes only the first healthy/fault pair in each split (`corpus.py:124-125`): context, arguments and replay, three of seven dependency groups. It does not stress the authority family containing the two false clears. Recomputed results: reverse options and irrelevant context each preserve all 6 judgments but only 4/6 are correct; equivalent wording preserves 5/6 and is correct on 5/6; removed evidence and wrong-subject each yield 6/6 correct composed unresolved judgments. The parallel raw-response recomputation finds all 12 underlying judgment choices are `contradicts`; the final safe action comes from applicability/sufficiency composition, not a correct raw judgment. Healthy arguments remain unresolved and healthy replay remains wrongly contradicted under the first two transformations. Stability is not accuracy. Equivalent wording makes one beneficial change, so an invariance failure need not be harmful. Thirty transformations add zero independent incidents. Adaptive attack budget is zero.

All eight missing cases have adverse outcomes. This does not prove leakage—the outcomes are withheld from initial payloads and no group-crossing was found—but it deprives the routing/economics study of matched missing-but-healthy controls and encourages overgeneralizing investigation value. Add those cases prospectively rather than reweighting or relabeling historical evidence.

### M07 — Actual action evidence does not support observed operational improvement

The action path exercises material/cosmetic context in fresh Application/SQLite state (`probes.py:388+`, `CORPUS.md:83-90`). Twenty-three executions are repeated policies over one context group, not 23 independent end-to-end scenarios. All finish with the correct acceptance state, so there is no observed comparative improvement in that subset. The authority false clears, escalation label improvements and other losses are scored actions under declared outcome keys and assumed economic scenarios; they are not recorded production losses or measured prevented harm. This limitation constrains favorable and unfavorable economic claims equally.

Minimum additional evidence: controlled trajectories where the compared decision actually changes later state in the affected family, downstream acceptance/delivery/effect metrics, and owner-relevant exposure/repair/review costs. Learning transfer of a deterministic guard is useful evidence, but without a measured pre/post or randomized learning intervention it is not causal learning uplift or monetized ROI.

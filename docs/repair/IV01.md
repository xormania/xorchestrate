# IV01: current inspection policy on historical aggregates

Every application-owned record in a public aggregate is inspected under the
current caller, requested inspection action, and that record's actual resource
bindings. A historical native source or an otherwise permitted work does not
authorize a denied nested session, assignment, evidence or contribution.
Authorized projections remain available. Stored history and trusted receipt or
acceptance inputs remain unchanged.

The independent witness read `get_work` while unrestricted, then denied
inspection of a private actor's session on the same work. The former historical
result schema omitted `get_work`, so the nested session leaked despite valid
direct denial and historical `get_delivery` redaction controls. The exact
unmodified witness reproduces on `ef7567516520543970af55b6f19534cb66eda357`.

## Implementation

`delivery.py` declares typed result fields for work/acceptance aggregates and
other application results. Projection recursively follows these structures,
including native-history results, preserved operation results, dispatch result
envelopes and message routing provenance. It evaluates the configured policy for
each record and marks changed public records with `inspection_redacted`.
Session/capability evidence uses its typed source envelope when resolving scope.
The selected delivery message receives the same projection as other messages,
while denial of that selected message still rejects the request.

Current `get_work`, `get_acceptance` and `get_operation` use the same projection. This closes the
direct sibling where a work-level grant formerly bypassed a same-action denial
on its nested records. Both reads recheck their authorization contexts and the
entire aggregate snapshot after policy evaluation. Concurrent nested updates or
deletions cannot return a mixture of authorized and changed records. Missing
resources in historical results are omitted when their bindings cannot be
reauthorized.

Focused adversarial review of the first repair found two additional bindings:
invocations and measurements did not carry their operation's provider into
inspection, and direct `get_operation` returned its typed result without the
historical projection. The expanded repair resolves those operation links and
uses the retained capability descriptor, including its annotations. Historical
executed invoke/measurement requests receive that same scope; an invoke without
a result still identifies its provider and qualified capability by its typed
address. Historical capability catalogs project their saved descriptors under
the requested inspection action. Inspection never discovers or invokes a
provider. Denial of a selected operation still rejects the whole request.

Only explicit application-owned result structures are traversed. Provider
outputs, caller evidence, session specifications and configured policy payloads
remain opaque; arbitrary keys named `work` or `sessions` are not reinterpreted as
store records. The policy still decides what the containing record may expose.
Derived acceptance summaries keep their original meaning; a redacted projection
is not a replacement for the complete trusted acceptance input.

## Sustained regressions and calibration

`tests/hexagonal/test_inspection_history.py` covers both MemoryStore and SQLite:

- The original same-work denied-session case, nested native-history depth, and
  historical `get_work`, `get_acceptance`, `get_delivery` and `list_sessions`.
- Direct work/acceptance projections, authorized records, policy restoration,
  unchanged stored observations and trusted work snapshots.
- Explicit same-action actor-record denial in selected/historical message
  routing snapshots; typed session-operation and dispatch result projections.
- Deletion and concurrent authorization changes, plus harmless user/provider
  payloads whose keys resemble application records.
- A sustained known-bad subject omits historical session traversal, verifies the
  resulting disclosure, and requires `IV01_HISTORICAL_RECORD_SCOPE` to fail.
  Direct denial and authorized projections remain controls. An unrelated
  projection `TimeoutError` propagates and receives no assertion-detection credit.
- Provider/name/descriptor-annotation denial for invocation and measurement
  history, direct work/acceptance measurements, and mixed historical capability
  catalogs; public payloads and exact history restoration remain valid controls.
- Direct and historical operation-result projections obey the same nested
  session policy. A second known-bad subject omits the provider binding and must
  fail `IV01_PROVIDER_SCOPE`; unrelated scope errors do not earn detection.

The copied new oracles fail at the disclosure assertion on the reviewed baseline
(10 failures, four valid controls). After repair, the unchanged independent
witness exits one at its old assertion that a leak must exist. A separately
retained two-line expectation adaptation verifies absence on both stores while
preserving all original controls. The original witness and raw baseline remain
unchanged; their copies, adaptation diff, raw outputs and command records are in
the repair evidence package's `iv01/` directory.

The initial component closure at `039446e` predates the provider and direct
operation counterexamples. Its identity, closure, manifest and replay documents
are preserved under `iv01/superseded-039446e/`; it does not receive credit for
the expanded closure. The adversarial scripts and their before/after raw
observations are retained separately under `adversarial/iv01/`.
